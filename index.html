Who to notify and how, isolation type and key periods for each condition. Sources: MOH list of notifiable diseases (KSA 2023), MOH Communicable Diseases Guidelines (2024), CDC isolation precautions (Appendix A) and the IPC Rapid Response Team list.

What each precaution means

Colors match common isolation door signs: blue Airborne, red Droplet, green Contact, grey Standard, purple Special. Neither WHO nor CDC sets official sign colors, so check your facility's signage.
